## A note on evidence quality in this field

Sexual health research is under-funded relative to its importance, dominated by self-report, prone to both moral and advocacy bias, and often based on small samples. Several of the most-cited studies in popular discourse (the Jiang testosterone paper, the Park pornography review, the Haake immune study) are weak. This guide has tried to weight the evidence honestly, note where claims rest on thin ground, and distinguish clinical consensus from folklore in either direction. Readers who encounter confident claims elsewhere — for or against masturbation, pornography, abstinence, or any technique — are encouraged to ask: how big was the sample, was it controlled, who funded it, and does the conclusion match the data?
